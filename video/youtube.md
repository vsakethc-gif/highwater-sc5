## Title

HighWater – Congressional App Challenge 2026, SC-5

## Description

HighWater is a free app that shows people in South Carolina's 5th Congressional District the flood and weather dangers near them right now, and what to do about them. It's built for everyone in our 8 counties (Cherokee, Chester, Fairfield, Kershaw, Lancaster, Lee, Union and York), especially older adults and families without a car.

It combines live public data (National Weather Service alerts, USGS river gauges, NOAA flood stages, FEMA flood zones and U.S. Census data) using written, published rules. It does not use AI to make decisions. Scenario Mode replays real archived data from Hurricane Helene, always labeled SIMULATION.

HighWater is not an official warning system. Follow local officials. In an emergency, call 911.

Try it: https://vsakethc-gif.github.io/highwater-sc5/
Source code: https://github.com/vsakethc-gif/highwater-sc5

Built with TypeScript, React Native and Expo, Vite, MapLibre, Supabase and Cloudflare Workers.
